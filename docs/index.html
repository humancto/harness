harness.

A local-first agent mesh for the laptops, desktops, and servers you already own. One Rust binary. Auto-discovers peers. Elects a brain. Routes work by capability. Cloud is escalation, not baseline.

The default for an AI workflow today is to send your data to someone else's GPU. We think the machines you already own can do most of it.

01
§ I — manifesto

Five things we believe before we write a line of code.

01

The planner runs locally.

Cloud LLMs are escalation, not baseline. They require a cloud_ok tag and policy approval. Their cost is surfaced in the audit log. Your laptop is the default.

02

Cardinality is first-class.

Every capability declares Anyone, Owner, or Federated. The dispatcher routes automatically; the brain doesn't override.

03

Idle compute is theft.

Every machine you own is at 5% utilization. Embed 100k docs. Grade 5k LLM outputs. Triage 50 issues. The cost of a mesh of your own hardware approaches zero.

04

Trust by pubkey.

QUIC + mutual cert pinning. Ed25519 identity per node. Pairing codes for first join. peers.toml on disk. No CA, no IdP, no third party.

05

One binary. No broker.

~30 MB stripped. SQLite + WAL at ~/.harness/harness.db. No Redis, no Postgres-as-a-service, no Kafka. Install with curl|sh; uninstall with rm.

Most workflows don't need someone else's GPU. They need your machines, working in concert.

— PRD §1, the pitch
